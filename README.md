# 🔍 My Analytics Approach

I generally approach a business problem through the following steps:

**Business Problem**
↓
**Data Collection**
↓
**Data Cleaning & Validation**
↓
**Exploratory Analysis**
↓
**KPI & Trend Analysis**
↓
**Dashboard / Visualization**
↓
**Business Insights**
↓
**Recommendations**

The goal is not only to create charts, but to understand **what the data is saying and why it matters to the business.**
